Items marked ✅ are shipped. 🔄 in progress. ⬜ planned.

## 🔄 In Progress

- **Game Results V2** — richer post-game screen with per-question breakdown, XP gained animation, share buttons
- **Royalty claim UI** — one-tap claim button on profile page with on-chain receipt

---

## ⬜ Planned — Near Term

- **Tournament mode** — bracket-style 8-player single-elimination, fixed CELO entry
- **Question sets** — curated themed packs (e.g. "World Capitals", "African Wildlife") playable as a unit
- **Contributor analytics** — dashboard showing plays, earnings, and engagement per submitted photo
- **Streak bonuses** — daily play streaks unlock XP multipliers and free entry tickets
- **Push notifications** — wallet-native alerts when a PvP opponent joins your session

---

## ⬜ Planned — Mid Term

- **Mobile app (PWA)** — installable web app with offline question cache and push notification support
- **Social profiles** — follow other players, view their public match history and question portfolio
- **Multi-round tournaments** — weekly on-chain tournaments with prize pool funded by entry fees
- **Governance** — cUSD-staked voting on question pool curation policy and fee parameters
- **Question NFTs** — mint a contributor's accepted question as an NFT; royalties follow the NFT

---

## 💡 Ideas Under Consideration

- Team duels (2v2 simultaneous)
- Time-limited flash events with boosted payouts
- Cross-chain question bridging (Base, Arbitrum)
- Farcaster Frame integration for viral loop
